OUR PURPOSE

A Simple Tool for Exploring Gematria Values

GematriaCalc is an online calculator created to make numerical letter-value calculations simple and accessible.

Instead of calculating letter values manually, users can enter a word, name, phrase, or sentence and select a supported gematria method. The calculator then produces the corresponding numerical value.

The website is designed to provide a straightforward way to explore how different gematria systems assign numerical values to letters.
